## Correctness and conceptual fit

- Motive content is separate from its actor/view-scoped MotivationHypothesis.
  Broad prompting reasons apply to any Entity actor; conscious intention is not
  required. Internal reasons, instructions and physical influences can be described
  without making human/mental/legal identity an admission gate.
- One motive/view is declared per hypothesis; actor RoleBinding is optional and
  derives its Entity from the selected interpretation. This permits source-only
  candidates and unresolved actors rather than manufactured people/observations.
  The six imported class references and exact event dependency are checked.
- Cited and uncited support remain distinct. Positive/negative support is independent;
  neither, positive, negative and both describe support presence, not truth.
  Several motives can coexist. Review/rejection is separate proposal workflow.
- Actual consequence derives through the admitted known view/record and optional
  exact Delta. No replacement event/state/actor fields or direct chosen Delta are
  introduced. Goals, intention, omission grounds, fault and sanction are not inferred.
- Prompting Entity/Fact/view anchors provide contextual declarations; timing,
  identity/episode alignment and semantic relevance remain manual constraints.
