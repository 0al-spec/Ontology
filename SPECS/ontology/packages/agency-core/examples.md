# Agency examples — domain-neutral, source-only

These are constructed illustrations of representation, not psychological findings
or normative classifications. Sources/review do not establish factual truth.

**Unrecognized motive, adopted intention.** A story attributes a need for recognition
to a person who does not recognize that motive. A distinct IntentionHypothesis
attributes commitment to a stated action. Awareness targets the exact scoped
MotivationHypothesis. The intention's support/review does not confirm the motive
or its unawareness. If the story merely omits an explanation, no awareness claim
is created.

**Desired but unrealized situation.** A driver wants to arrive before an appointment.
`GoalHypothesis(direction=seek)` targets the content "arrive before appointment".
An intention may concern a particular route. Neither creates a record that the
driver took the route or arrived on time. Physical states and actual Delta remain
unknown unless independently supplied.

**Anticipated but unwanted situation.** A driver anticipates that a vehicle could
roll, without desiring that outcome. Anticipation(possible) and attitude(opposes)
are separate claims. A different account can attribute acceptance instead; no
normative label follows from either. The machine's movement and the driver's
omission remain different contribution scopes.

**Maintenance without change.** A community seeks to keep an access route clear.
Goal(maintain) targets that condition; a positive outcome may require no change
in the chosen projection. Unknown physical changes are still not an empty diff.

**Nonhuman actor without assumed psychology.** An AI may be described as pursuing
a configured condition, with separate claims about its goals/anticipation if the
interpretation explicitly uses those concepts. A physical reading of a rolling
car may contain only a broad motivating influence from motivation-core and no
agency hypotheses at all. The absence does not assert an unconscious mind or
automatically create not_applicable.

**Late report, earlier or unknown stance.** A source reports at time R about an
actor's stance at time P. The two declarations retain distinct clock/instant
pairs; if P is unavailable, R never fills it. Different clocks are not compared
without an explicit external mapping. A claimed expectation or intention is not
dated automatically from a later actual outcome.

**Opposed accounts.** Two source assertions may support and oppose the same goal
claim. Both support lists survive review. A third assertion about anticipation
has its own links; approving the goal does not approve the forecast. The same
source may be explicitly cited by both, but relevance for each remains separate.
